# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Subject Matter Experts (SME)

### Role Summary
Subject Matter Experts provide specialized domain knowledge and expertise to inform planning, design, and delivery decisions. They ensure solutions align with business, technical, or compliance requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Advise teams on business, technical, or compliance requirements
- Review deliverables for accuracy and alignment with specialized requirements
- Participate in key design reviews, planning sessions, and checkpoints
- Identify risks related to domain expertise
- Mentor team members on specialized knowledge

### Goals
- Ensure quality and correctness of solutions within their domain
- Reduce rework and compliance risks
- Transfer knowledge across the team

### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions and technical discussions
- Requirement clarification and acceptance criteria refinement
- Ad-hoc consultations during implementation

### Interactions
- Collaborates with Product Manager to refine requirements and acceptance criteria
- Consults with Developers during implementation and code reviews
- Partners with QA to define domain-specific test scenarios

---

## UX Designers

### Role Summary
UX Designers ensure the product delivers a high-quality, user-centered experience. They conduct research, create designs, and validate solutions through user feedback.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Collaborate on accessibility and inclusive design
- Review design implementation for fidelity and usability
- Advocate for user needs throughout the project lifecycle

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce user friction and support burden
- Measure and improve user satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Design critiques and usability testing sessions
- Design specifications and component documentation
- Collaboration with Product Managers on feature prioritization

### Interactions
- Collaborates with Product Manager for feature ideation and requirements
- Works with Developers to translate designs into implementation
- Partners with QA to validate user experience in testing

---

## DevOps Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers bridge development and operations by managing infrastructure, automation, and deployment pipelines. They enable fast, reliable delivery and operational excellence.

### Responsibilities
- Implement and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Manage infrastructure, configuration, and deployment automation
- Monitor systems, logs, and alerts for operational health
- Assist in incident response and root cause analysis
- Advise on scalability, reliability, and security improvements

### Goals
- Enable rapid, safe deployments
- Maintain high system reliability and performance
- Reduce deployment friction and operational overhead

### Typical Communication
- Infrastructure planning meetings and release coordination
- On-call incident response and post-mortems
- Performance and reliability dashboards and reviews

### Interactions
- Supports Developers for local development environments and deployment automation
- Works with Project Manager for scheduling releases and deployment windows
- Collaborates with QA on test environment setup and management
- Partners with Product Manager on performance monitoring and capacity planning

---

## QA / Testing

### Role Summary
QA and Testing professionals ensure product quality by validating acceptance criteria, identifying defects, and verifying that solutions meet business and user needs.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans and acceptance criteria validation
- Conduct manual and automated testing
- Identify, triage, and document defects
- Perform regression and smoke testing
- Validate fixes and sign off on quality gates

### Goals
- Ensure high product quality and user satisfaction
- Reduce production defects and rework
- Accelerate delivery through efficient testing

### Typical Communication
- Test planning sessions and acceptance criteria review
- Defect reports and status dashboards
- Quality gate reviews and release sign-offs

### Interactions
- Works with Product Manager to understand acceptance criteria and success metrics
- Collaborates with Developers on testability and test coverage
- Partners with UX Designers on user experience validation
- Coordinates with DevOps on test environment management
